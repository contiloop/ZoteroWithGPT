# Selected local study

The researcher manually saves a paper/PDF, selects the ChatGPT plugin, and gives
one arXiv link. Local discovery plus actual PDF reading establishes the target;
Web abstracts and parent metadata cannot establish its bytes or version.

This removes acquisition from a study-save request and avoids depending on
inconsistent automatic plugin activation. Missing files require manual action.
Unknown versions stay unknown; ambiguity requires a focused choice.

Keep the paper only in accessible conversation context. Comparisons do not
switch destinations; a failed explicit switch suspends the prior destination.
A later clear save uses that target without demanding repeated internal details.

Follow the requested output: organize, summarize, or copy as asked. Preserve
meaning and requested mathematical detail; whole-conversation byte copying is
not the default. Long content remains beside its passage. Native readback,
manual-edit fencing, and historical retry protection remain necessary.
